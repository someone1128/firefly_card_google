## 英文版本 (Primary)

**Name:** Streamer Card

**Summary (150 chars max):**
Transform selected text into beautiful visual cards. Support Twitter, Weibo, Jike content beautification with one-click conversion.

**Description:**

🎨 **Beautiful Visual Card Creator for Social Media Content**

Streamer Card transforms your selected text and social media posts into stunning visual cards with just one click. Perfect for Microsoft Edge users who want to enhance their content sharing experience.

⚡ **Key Features:**
• **Seamless Edge Integration** - Optimized specifically for Microsoft Edge browser
• **Universal Text Conversion** - Select any text and create beautiful cards instantly
• **Social Platform Support** - Native support for Twitter/X, Weibo, and Jike
• **Professional Templates** - Multiple design templates with modern aesthetics
• **Rich Text Support** - Full Markdown formatting capabilities
• **Image Preservation** - Automatically includes images from original posts
• **Clean Output** - No watermarks or unwanted branding
• **Privacy-Focused** - No data collection, works entirely offline

🌍 **Multi-Language Support:**
English, 中文 (Chinese), 日本語 (Japanese), 한국어 (Korean), Español, Français, Deutsch, and more

🚀 **How to Use:**
1. Install the extension in Microsoft Edge
2. Navigate to any website or social media platform  
3. Select text or click on supported social posts
4. Right-click and choose "Streamer Card" from context menu
5. Edit and customize your beautiful visual card

🎯 **Perfect for Edge Users Who Are:**
• Content creators and digital marketers
• Social media enthusiasts
• Students and professionals
• Anyone looking to beautify their text sharing

🔐 **Security & Privacy:**
• Microsoft Edge compatible and secure
• No personal data collection
• Open source transparency
• Client-side processing only

Enhance your Microsoft Edge browsing experience with beautiful content creation!
