## 英文版本 (Primary)

**Title:** Streamer Card | Social Media Content Beautifier

**Short Description (132 chars max):**
Transform text into beautiful cards. Support Twitter, Weibo, Jike content beautification with one click.

**Detailed Description:**

🎨 **Transform Your Content Into Stunning Visual Cards**

Streamer Card is a powerful browser extension that transforms selected text and social media posts into beautifully designed visual cards with just one click. Perfect for content creators, social media enthusiasts, and anyone who wants to make their sharing more visually appealing.

✨ **Key Features:**
• **One-Click Text Transformation** - Select any text and right-click to create beautiful cards
• **Multi-Platform Support** - Works seamlessly with Twitter/X, Weibo, and Jike
• **Beautiful Templates** - Multiple design templates with gradients and color schemes  
• **Markdown Support** - Full Markdown syntax support for rich formatting
• **Image Integration** - Automatically includes images from social posts
• **No Watermarks** - Clean, professional-looking cards without branding
• **Customizable Display** - Control what information to show or hide

🌍 **Global Language Support:**
• English, 中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch

🔧 **How It Works:**
1. Browse any website or social media platform
2. Select text or click the extension button on supported posts
3. Automatically redirects to the card editor with your content pre-filled
4. Customize and share your beautiful visual card

🎯 **Perfect For:**
• Content creators and influencers
• Social media managers
• Students and educators
• Anyone who wants to beautify their text sharing

🔒 **Privacy & Security:**
• No data collection or storage
• Works entirely client-side
• Open source and transparent

Transform your text into visual masterpieces today!

**Keywords:** social media, content creation, text beautification, visual cards, Twitter, Weibo, design, markdown
